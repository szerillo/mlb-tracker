Ledger

Paper trades, 1 unit risked per bet at the entry price (Kalshi cost includes the fee). CLV is in the source's unit: probability points for prices, runs for total lines, % for the sheet-at-open moneyline. Beat close = the close moved toward our side (CLV > 0); unchanged closes are excluded from that rate. ROI over this kind of sample is noisy; CLV is the first read. "Sheet at the open" totals are priced at −110 because the open price is not archived. Models: Sheet at the open = your number vs the sportsbook open (qualifying = ML 3, total 2.5, F5 3.5 pts); Sides board = your number vs the best current price, with the Kalshi lag rule logged separately; Sharp Props board = the calls the props tab showed (first appearance); Prop scan = every scan flag by rule; My tickets = data/prop_scan/tickets.csv.
