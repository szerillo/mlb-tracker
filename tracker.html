Market performance — rolling over-rate

Share of games finishing over the listed total (pushes excluded). This is the honest market-miss signal: 50% = market perfectly centered. Bounces near 50 all season.

Run environment — actual R/G vs listed line

Rolling mean of actual runs/game against the mean listed total. The gap between them is right-skew (a few blowouts pull the mean above the median-scale line) — it is not a betting edge. Track the trend for offense/COR context, not for over value; use the over-rate chart above for that.
