Methodology notes · wRC+ vs RHP / LHP are weighted 3-year multi-year averages (FanGraphs splits leaderboard, Total = most recent 3 seasons) with empirical-Bayes shrinkage toward league mean (k=100 PAs) so small-sample rookies/call-ups don't skew rankings.
